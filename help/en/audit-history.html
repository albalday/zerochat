Review 4: Evolution from Git

911 commits in 48 days, from ChatCLI v0.9 to 9.1.0, and how long each rule took to get its test.

Method and limits

Facts are checked with git log, git show and git log -S on AGENTS.md; dates and versions come from each commit. Interpretations are marked as such. Most commits only have a title, so the history does not always record why a decision was made.

This review adds a question the 8.12.4 audit did not ask: when did each rule reach AGENTS.md, and when did the test that protects it arrive. It is the criterion of the 9.0.0 lesson.

1. Figures

  • 911 commits between 26 August and 12 October 2026, with activity on 46 of those 48 days: about 19 a day, peaking at 74 (02/09) and 65 (29/08).
  • 140 commits since the 8.12.4 audit (aff1019, 04/10): 114 up to the 9.0.0 release and 26 after it.
  • Commits list coding agents as co-authors (Claude Opus 5, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1); the documentation also mentions Codex and Antigravity. Interpretation: the pace fits development directed by instructions and carried out mostly by agents.

2. Phases

Origin and growth (26 August – 16 September)

ChatCLI v0.9 (39fed30), multichat and voice, later removed (cbc4cb1). AgentCore and the first MCP (969526e). Renamed to ZeroChat (ad107f4). Orama RAG and the first AGENTS.md on 02/09 (481c9c6, d37ff35). SVG icons, in-app dialogs and WebLLM (e20a70c, 11/09).

Backend and hardening (17 September – 4 October)

End of the bundle and unified zerochat.py in 7.0.0 (f58544f). PyPI and the modular backend in py/ (b3db5f0). HMAC signatures (c51d0a8). ChatState slices with their test (2059455) and the 0600 token. Audit and release of 8.12.4 (aff1019).

8.12 series (4 – 9 October)

From 8.12.5 to 8.12.16: the audit findings closed with tests for empty catch blocks and untranslated literals, branch summaries, project mode and its protocol.

Towards 9.0.0 (9 – 11 October)

Agent with no step limit and loop detection (38ef6f9). run_command and check_command replace bash and execute_command (514591d). Read-only subagents (ba21510). Reasoning replay between steps (a3e41b6). First cleanup of dead and compatibility code (c1c66af, 8d769f7) and, the day of the release, the rules and tests against dead and duplicated code (f785a3f). 9.0.0 released on 11/10 (d2ab109).

The cleanup (11 – 12 October)

Tests with a real DOM (c182c38) and 17 breaking refactors. The largest: generation and conversation (6bea226, −5,318 lines), tools (63d4315, −3,337) and MCP (95ab896, −2,840). The style (26abb4c) and backend (72deff7) tests arrive. In total, in js/, css/ and py/: −16,522 and +7,637 lines.

Interpretation: the only rule that came with its test on the same day from the start (dialogs) created no debt. Those that waited almost a month for their test piled up tens or hundreds of violations, which surfaced all at once when the test arrived. Those that were not even written allowed the largest debt in the project. Debt grew with time without a test, not with how hard the rule was.

4. What the history adds

  • The current scope is the result of removals: voice, avatars, the bundle, Tree-RAG, bash/execute_command and the agent's turn limit were all removed.
  • Audits age fast: the 8.12.4 one described agent_checkpoint, update_plan, finish_task and a loop of 5 to 200 turns; a week later none of them existed.
  • Releasing a major version is not the same as finishing it: 9.0.0 shipped with its first hygiene test and needed 26 more commits the next day.
  • Rules follow problems: almost all of them arrive after the problem exists. Interpretation: without a test to stop it, an agent reintroduces what the rule forbids.

Conclusion: 9.1.0 is cleaner than any earlier version, and for the first time most rules have a test. Those still without one are listed in Review 1.
