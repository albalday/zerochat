What is ZeroChat?

ZeroChat is a standalone, secure, and portable web client designed to interact with artificial intelligence models, orchestrate autonomous multi-turn agents, and search local document knowledge bases.

Unlike conventional web apps, ZeroChat is distributed as a single HTML file (zerochat.html) that runs directly via the file:// protocol or any static web server with zero external dependencies, database backends, or proxies. All your personal data, chat histories, profile credentials, and indexed documents remain strictly on your machine within your browser.

Download zerochat.html (Standalone Bundle) Ready to run locally with a double click (file://) or deploy to your own server.
Privacy by design: ZeroChat collects no telemetry and connects to no central servers. Your queries travel directly from your browser to your configured provider or run 100% locally on your GPU via WebGPU.
